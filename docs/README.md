# Documentation

Study guidance, literature reviews, research plans, and archived research documents
are centralized under `docs/`. Repository and component `README.md` files and
generated result reports remain beside the code, data, results, or submission package
they describe.

## Maintained workflow

- [Study design](study.md): current C/G/R question, arms, budgets, and statistical units.
- [Reproduction](reproduction.md): commands, inputs, and existing result destinations.
- [Data contract](data_contract.md): identities, arrays, units, and prediction boundaries.
- [Dataset schema](dataset_schema.md): manifest fields and scientific data conventions.
- [Rendering](rendering.md): numerical implementation and qualification requirements.
- [HST experiment plan](hst_experiment_plan.md) and [implementation](hst_study.md):
  proposed observational transfer and its preparation gates.
- [Migration](migration.md): maintained ownership and retired workflow boundaries.
- [Artifact policy](artifact_policy.md): tracking, provenance, and submission rules.

## Literature and planning

- [`literature_review/`](literature_review/): evidence reviews, source records, and
  data-access assessments. These are background and planning materials, not executable
  experiment specifications.
- The [eight-page paper plan](eight_page_paper_plan.md) records the current manuscript
  scope. Other proposals are marked by date and status in their own documents.
- [`archive/`](archive/): dated, superseded, and historical documentation, including
  its supporting manifests and registries. Do not treat its old commands or paths as
  the maintained workflow.
