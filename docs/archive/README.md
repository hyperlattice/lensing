# Archived documentation

This directory holds dated, superseded, and historical Markdown records and their
small supporting manifests or registries. The material was moved here from the
repository-level `archive/docs/` and `archive/root/` locations so literature and
planning documents have one home under `docs/`.

Use the [documentation index](../README.md) for the maintained workflow. Historical
paths and results described here remain records of their original checkout; they are
not instructions to rerun those workflows. Archived scripts, configurations, and
notebooks remain in the [repository archive](../../archive/README.md).
