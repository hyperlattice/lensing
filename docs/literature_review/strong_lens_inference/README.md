# Strong-lens inference literature review

Review cutoff: **4 November 2026**. Scope: automated galaxy-scale lens mass inference, especially Einstein radius, observation/simulation mismatch, uncertainty and real-data validation.

- [Review and proposed scientific direction](review.md)
- [Readable bibliography](references.md): 60 unique topical scholarly works, with status and access depth.
- [Evidence matrix](evidence_matrix.csv): one row per work; design, finding, limitation, project implication and primary source.
- [Verified metadata](references.json) and [BibTeX](references.bib).
- [Claim-to-source map](claim_source_map.json).
- [Existing manuscript citation audit](citation_audit.md).
- [Exact discovery queries](search_log.json), [coverage and limitations](coverage.json), and [local provenance](provenance.md).

The PDF is exported separately under `output/pdf/strong_lens_inference_review.pdf` and combines the review, bibliography and citation audit. The machine-readable evidence matrix remains a companion artifact.

The 60 include direct inference studies, classical methods, software, astrophysical context, adjacent selection/detection studies and general methods. They are not 60 independent empirical lens-inference studies. Ten received selected full-text assessment; other records were assessed at abstract/landing-page level. No formal systematic-review completeness or pooled accuracy is claimed. The HOLISMOKES correction is tracked separately with unresolved full-text access.

The manuscript, its bibliography and experiment code are unchanged. No new training or scoring was performed. The review's workflow attribution is recorded separately from the topical evidence.
